MyTirri – Har Safar Ka Saathi
Version: MVP v1
Prepared For: Project Discussion
1. Vision
MyTirri is a local transport discovery platform connecting passengers with nearby
vehicle owners. The first version focuses on discovery, contact, and trust rather than
online booking.
2. Goals
• Easy search of local transport.
• City and Mohalla based listings.
• Free registration for Captains.
• Simple interface for local users.
• Build a trusted local transport directory.
3. User Types
Captain – Driver or Vehicle Owner.
User – Customer searching for transport.

14. Why MyTirri?
Unlike large ride-hailing apps, MyTirri focuses on local transport availability. Users can
easily find nearby drivers based on city and mohalla, making it especially useful in
Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns where local transport is fragmented.
